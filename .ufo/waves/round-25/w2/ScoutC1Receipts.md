## Findings

   yields `RECEIPT-COUNTS-ABSENT` (exit 2) exactly as amendment 3 §5 discloses. The refusal control
   can never verify to exit 0 — by design, not a defect.
2. `coverage-agreement-at-base.tsv` verifies fully (`verify=ok`, exit 0).
3. Wave-1 C1 red/after receipts (7 of 8) are **not verifiable**: they bind tool paths that do not
   exist at their recorded rev (w1 drafts untracked) or are scratch paths outside the repo
   (`RECEIPT-TOOL-ABSENT`). Only `c1-r24-fixture-on-draft.tsv` (committed fixture) verifies.
   These are uncommitted wave evidence, not the frozen candidate; any C1 after/red receipt must be
   regenerated by the runner against a committed tool blob at the landing tip to be verifiable.
4. No C1 after/red receipt exists in the committed census (`census/round-25/records/` holds only the
   five base controls). The wave-2 claim therefore has no committed after-receipt yet — expected at
   this stage of the round.
