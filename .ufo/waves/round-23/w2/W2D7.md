## What Q54 covers, and what it does not

Covers: a locator whose token appears nowhere in the Gate's declared snapshot text (the
`ZQXJNOSUCH` shape). That is the one case the fixture can fail.

Does not cover, and therefore leaves D7 open:
1. a locator naming a section the snapshot lacks when the word occurs anywhere in prose (M17 itself);
2. a locator's second word — the option/flag after the section (`--no-copy`, `--unreachable`) is
   never examined; only the first whitespace-delimited token is;
3. invented hyphenated sections;
4. per-row attribution — `SNAPSHOT_RE` unions every `snapshot:` in the Gate, so a locator may resolve
   against a *different row's* file;
5. its own check cell's greenness on the lane (17 bad at tip).
