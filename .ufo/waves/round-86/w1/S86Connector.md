1. **Reuse:** It executes an isolated Python subprocess (`sys.executable -I -B -c bootstrap`) that compiles and executes `tools/souji-receipt-write.py` into a dynamic module `souji_receipt_write`, directly invoking the writer's `_truth_refusal(root)`, `_flag_refusal(root, tag, p)`, and `git()` helper.
