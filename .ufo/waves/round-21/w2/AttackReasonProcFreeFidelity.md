The baseline reason *adapter* is assembled in the fixture rather than extracted as a named function (`:30-44`). This corresponds to baseline census construction at 6ff3413 `:347-368` (path `sanitize_field`, reason assignment, then whole-reason sanitization before row emission); the extra whole-reason sanitization is idempotent after path sanitization. Therefore the adapter preserves the baseline reason-field bytes for these cases, though it is not a literal extraction of the full census emitter.

Bounded oracle caveat: the fixture does not itself assert that the supplied `--base` file is the 6ff3413 blob or verify its digest; exactness is conditional on the caller supplying that source. `Path.read_text()` also makes this a source-text extraction, not an independently checked byte-for-byte digest. No fixture invocation or result was observed in this assignment.
