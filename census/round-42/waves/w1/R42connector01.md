## 5. Verdict

Overlap exists at the level of concept and vocabulary, not of code. Souji's `souji-freeze-check.py`, its C3 counter and its C1 register do not duplicate any sibling tool, and no sibling emits souji's finding classes. The collisions to record are: the `freeze-check` name clash with chousa (F1), the FROZEN-EDITED edit-then-restore gap against kaidoku's round-45 `restored` arm (F2), the empty-pin-map defect family shared with kaidoku (F3), the tameshi mutation method for C3 (F4), and the absence of any fleet shared freeze/prereg utility (F5). All five are reported only; nothing was edited, and the frozen instruments were not run.
