## Findings for the L1 (defects in the frozen counter; reported, not edited)

- **F-A (boundary, new_mismatches):** the counter tests `r[0] > len(base_lines)`. `base_lines` is `split("\n")` of a newline-terminated file, so its length is 840 for 839 lines; the first appended SCORECARD line is 840 and is not counted. Evidence: scenario A06 appends one unresolvable row as line 840. The instrument reports it: `python3 -B tools/souji-declared-evidence.py --root <clone> --rev <rev> --file SCORECARD.md --format tsv` prints `840	zz-r42-absent-path.md	A	mismatch:path-absent…` and `mismatches=71`, while the counter prints `new_mismatches=0`. M50 (`>=`) prints `new_mismatches=1` on A06 and is killed.
