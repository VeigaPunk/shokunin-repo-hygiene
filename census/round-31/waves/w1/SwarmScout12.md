## Findings
Quotes are verbatim from bytes opened this run; line numbers are the raw file lines.

1. **What `reflog expire` removes** — git-reflog.adoc:66-68: "Entries older than `expire` time, or entries older than `expire-unreachable` time and not reachable from the current tip, are removed from the reflog." It prunes entries, not the reflog itself: git-reflog.adoc:62-63 "The "drop" subcommand completely removes the reflog for the specified references." and :63-64 "both of which can be used to delete reflog entries, but not the reflog itself." :69-70: "This is typically not used directly by end users -- instead, see linkgit:git-gc[1]."
2. **`--expire` vs `--expire-unreachable`** — git-reflog.adoc:111-112 "`--expire=<time>`: Prune entries older than the specified time." and :119-121 "`--expire-unreachable=<time>`: Prune entries older than `<time>` that are not reachable from the current tip of the branch." Special values: :115-117 "`--expire=all` prunes entries regardless of their age; `--expire=never` turns off pruning of reachable entries (but see `--expire-unreachable`)"; :124-127 "`--expire-unreachable=all` prunes unreachable entries regardless of their age; `--expire-unreachable=never` turns off early pruning of unreachable entries" (`but see --expire`). Consequence for the fixture: `--expire=all` alone removes every entry; `--expire=never --expire-unreachable=all` removes only entries whose commit is not reachable from the ref's current tip.
3. **Default ages** — git-reflog.adoc:113-115 "the expiration time is taken from the configuration setting `gc.reflogExpire`, which in turn defaults to 90 days." and :122-124 "`gc.reflogExpireUnreachable`, which in turn defaults to 30 days." Same in config/gc.adoc:117-118 "'git reflog expire' removes reflog entries older than this time; defaults to 90 days." (gc.reflogExpire, :115-122) and :126-128 "removes reflog entries older than this time and are not reachable from the current tip; defaults to 30 days." (gc.reflogExpireUnreachable, :124-132). gc.adoc:134-138 explains why unreachable is shorter: "These types of entries are generally created as a result of using `git commit --amend` or `git rebase`". Both accept "now" (expire all immediately) and "never" (config/gc.adoc:118-119, :128-129); per-ref form `gc.<pattern>.reflogExpire`.
4. **Reflog entries keep objects from being pruned** — NOT in git-reflog.adoc or config/gc.adoc as a direct statement. Located in git-gc.adoc NOTES (:138-146): "objects referenced by the index, remote-tracking branches, reflogs (which may reference commits in branches that were later amended or rewound)" (:144-146; lead sentence :141-142 "'git gc' tries very hard not to delete objects that are referenced anywhere in your repository."). This is the text S063 records (§ NOTES first paragraph). Only indirect support inside git-reflog.adoc: :147-148 (`--stale-fix`) "garbage collecting using older versions of Git, which didn't protect objects referred to by reflogs." — shows reflogs protect objects in current git, but it is not the primary statement. Companion limits: git-gc.adoc:160-161 "Any object with modification time newer than the `--prune` date is kept, along with everything reachable from it." and config/gc.adoc:96-99 gc.pruneExpire default 2 weeks.
5. **Also relevant to the entry's "what removes it" side**: `--stale-fix` (git-reflog.adoc:139-143) prunes entries pointing at broken commits; `git reflog expire` removes entries only — objects go later at prune time (git-prune/gc grace, S117/S002), so an entry that cites `expire` must not claim `expire` deletes the object.
