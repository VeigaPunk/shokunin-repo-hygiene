## 5. Connector move (one bounded proposal)

**Evidence.** 47 subprocess calls under `census/round-8*/`+`round-9*/` have no deadline (45 of them Python
fixture/instrument children; `lane-pattern.txt`, §2a), including the frozen r97 chain: search→harness,
harness→counter (4 sites) + `git clone`, shapes→shape (2 sites), forgery-controls→search/shapes (2 sites). A hung counter, a blocked `git clone` or a sleeping shape stalls the
measurement with no receipt and no bound — the same class the C2 claim fixes for the probe. The remedy already
exists in this round's own bytes: `census/round-97/c2-forgery-probe.py:90-147` (`_wait_output`: selectors,
`--max-output`, distinct killed/timeout, `Fatal` → exit 2).

**Affected axes.** Operability 8: removes an unbounded stall from every measurement path. Calibration 7: no
change when nothing hangs; a deadline hit becomes a *new* distinct class. Coverage: none.

**Move (r98, never retro-fitted into r97's frozen bytes).** Lift `_wait_output` (with a per-layer bound and a
byte cap) into the C1 chain's child runs, and score a deadline hit as `timeout` — a class separate from
`errored`, `killed`, `hung` and the verdict exits — so a stalled run is countable and attributable instead of
invisible.
