99:**Findings:**
100:- **F1, medium:** blob reads have no size cap and no subprocess timeout (L116-158, L199-201).
101:  - A small compressed blob that inflates to gigabytes can OOM the gate or fill /var/tmp.
102:  - If the gate is OOM-killed, the cleanup in `finally` doesn't run, so the hostile bytes stay in /var/tmp.
103:  - **Fix:** check the size first (`cat-file -s` or `ls-tree -l`), treat oversize as could-not-evaluate (exit 2), and add `timeout=` to every call.
104:- **F2, medium-low:** two parts of the carry check can be driven into near-endless CPU time.
105:  - Each lesson line is re-normalised for every report needle (L336-337), so the work is M×N.
106:  - The `<!--.*?-->` regex (L85/L289) is quadratic when comments are never closed.
107:  - **Fix:** normalise the lesson lines once, and strip comments with a linear scan.
108:- **F3, low:** many pin keys pointing at one large blob mean one full read and hash per key. Blobs at the measured commit are hashed twice (L491/L502) and the record is read twice (L427/L458).
