## C2 — operability 8: the K4 delegation's FLAGFAIL detail is injective on the bytes and keeps the head on control bytes
Stated wording:
  In tools/souji-record-agreement.py, the FLAGFAIL branch no longer renders the writer's raw stderr through
  `backslashreplace` + `_one_line` (escape first, then the escaped-text head/tail bound). It decodes the base64
  frame with `surrogateescape`, prints each surrogate byte as `\\xNN` with its original value (so a bad byte and
  its literal spelling stay distinct), and bounds the RAW bytes first — head-100 / `…` / tail-99 of raw content —
  before escaping the window, so the head is spent on content, not on escape sequences. The resulting K4 detail is
  ONE physical line whose RAW window is at most 200 bytes; the escaped rendering can exceed 200 characters on
  control-heavy input (each control byte costs four), and that is stated, not hidden. A stderr carrying byte 0xFF

  bytes followed by `AttributeError: boom` still names `AttributeError` in the head; and every r88 case still
  passes. The frozen 10-case fixture tests/fixtures/r89-k4-flagfail.py reports `cases=10 tool_pass=10 before_pass=8`,
